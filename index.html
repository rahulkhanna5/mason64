The materials

Two woods that have been arguing beautifully for centuries.

American walnut

The dark squares and the frame. Close-grained, deep chocolate, and it darkens further with the years and the light in your room.

White oak

The light squares. Quarter-sawn so the ray fleck catches across the board, and hard enough to take a lifetime of pieces set down firmly.

Brass inlay

A single line of brass laid between the playing field and the frame. It is the only ornament on the board, and it is enough.

The pieces

Staunton proportions, cut a little cleaner.

The classical silhouette every player already knows, with the fussier turnings taken out. Weighted, felted, and lacquered in gloss black or ivory. A king that sits properly in the hand and refuses to tip.
